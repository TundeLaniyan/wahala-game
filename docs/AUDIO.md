# WAHALA! audio: infrastructure, and what is (not) shipped

State on `claude/wahala-gritty-gameplay-v1`: the approved-playlist music system (`src/audio/playlist.ts`,
`src/audio/music.ts`, `src/audio/escalation.ts`) with **nine of the ten approved tracks installed and playing**.

## Read this first

The `claude/wahala-lagos-audio` branch produced generated Afrobeats music, synthetic Nigerian Pidgin voices and
generated "Lagos" ambience beds. **The founder rejected all three.** On this branch:

| Part | State |
| --- | --- |
| Generated Afrobeats music (`match_loop`, `menu_loop`, `match_tension`, stings) | **Rejected and removed.** Replaced by the founder's ten **approved Pixabay tracks** (see "Music: the approved playlist" below). **Nine are installed** (`public/audio/music/pixabay_<id>.mp3`); #7 (`pixabay_496406`) has not been supplied and its slot is skipped. |
| Synthetic Pidgin voice packs (Kokoro TTS) | **Rejected and removed.** `public/audio/voices/voices.json` keeps only the line catalogue (ids, categories, text) with `packs: {}`; no line can play until a real recording is listed in `recorded.json`. |
| Generated Lagos ambience beds (`crowd_loop`, `buspark_loop`, `crowd_loop_hype` files) | **Rejected and removed.** `public/audio/slots.json` is empty, so the beds fall back to the runtime-synthesised crowd beds that existed before the audio branch. |
| Generators (`tools/audiogen/`) | **Removed** so the rejected audio cannot be regenerated by accident. They remain in the history of `claude/wahala-lagos-audio`. |
| Infrastructure (volumes, music player, voice/reaction director, manifests, recording slots, lifecycle) | **Kept and tested.** Described below. |

`tests/audio/assets.test.ts` fails if any audio file other than the ten approved `music/pixabay_<id>.mp3` files is
added under `public/audio/`, if the music manifest lists anything but exactly those ten ids (with their source pages
and the Pixabay Content License), if a present music file has no row in `public/audio/LICENSES.md`, if voice packs,
slots or recordings are listed, or if `tools/audiogen/` comes back. Update that test deliberately when other real,
licensed audio is added.

Every sound that plays today is synthesised at runtime (`src/audio/recipes`): effects, warnings, engine and
generator loops, the crowd beds and crowd reactions. None of it is a recording; do not describe it as one. The only
recordings the game plays are the approved Pixabay music tracks.

## Layers and volumes

```
music   music.ts: streamed playlist track       -> musicDuck -> musicBus ----\
sfx     effects                                   -> sfxBus -------------------+
warn    danger warnings (never ducked)            -> warnBus (sfx volume) -----+-> master -> limiter -> out
react   crowd reactions + spoken lines            -> notch -> reactDuck -> crowdBus
amb     ambience beds                             -> notch -> ambDuck  -> crowdBus
```

`src/audio/prefs.ts` holds four independent persistent volumes (localStorage key `wahala.audio.v1`; corrupt or
unavailable storage falls back to the defaults) plus the voices on/off switch:

| Slider | Default | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master | 80% | everything |
| Music | 45% | the playlist track (silent until the approved files are added) |
| Ambience & voices | 65% | crowd beds, crowd reactions, spoken lines |
| Effects | 85% | synthesised effects and warnings |

`src/ui/AudioSettings.tsx` shows them on the title screen and the pause menu, with mute (stored in the existing
`GameSettings.muted`, shared with the HUD button), the voices switch and a reset. The engine follows the store
itself; the legacy `setVolumes()` is ignored while a store is active.

Lifecycle: nothing is created before the first user gesture (`unlock()`); `startMusic(arenaId)` enters the match
soundscape (and picks this match's track), `setPaused(bool)` follows the pause menu, `stopMusic()` leaves the match
(ambience fades, generator/engine loops stop, menu music if any), `dispose()` stops everything, unsubscribes from the
preferences and closes the context. All loaders resolve (never reject) and stay silent when files are missing or
fail to decode.

### Availability at runtime

After the first gesture the player fetches the manifest and probes each listed file once (a `HEAD` request; a
dev-server HTML fallback page does not count as a track). A file that is missing, or that later fails to play, is
skipped silently (the game logs nothing; the browser itself may still log the 404 of a probe). With zero files the
music is silent and everything else works. `whenMusicLoaded()` reports which ids were found.

### Rotation and arena playlists (`src/audio/playlist.ts`)

- Each match (`startMusic(arenaId)`, called by `MatchController.start` with `settings.arenaId`) picks a track from
  that arena's playlist, uniformly among the available ones, **never the previous match's track** while another one is
  available. Rematches therefore always change track once two or more files are present. The randomness is injected
  (`AudioOptions.musicRand`, seeded from the clock by default) so tests are deterministic.
- `playlists.default` lists all ten; `playlists.arenas.<arenaId>` gives an arena its own `tracks`, a `start` track and
  an optional `signature`. A signature (meant for when there are ten arenas, one track each) always plays on its arena
  while available. The `start` track opens the **first** match on that arena in a session (unless it was the track
  just heard); later matches rotate through the arena's list, never repeating the previous track.
- Today each arena has its own three tracks: Danfo Bus Park 504898 (start, ~117 BPM) / 406552 / 491432 (+ the empty
  496406 slot), the busiest and fastest; Balogun Market 573648 "summer groove" (start) / 569473 / 540950; Lekki
  Roadworks 491433 (start) / 588999 / 439622, the slower, heavier ones for the night shift. (Tempos are rough
  onset-autocorrelation estimates; the grouping has not been judged by ear.) Signatures are `null`.
- `menu` lists optional title-screen tracks. It is empty, so the menus stay silent (the tracks were approved for
  matches; add ids there to use some on the title screen).

### Streaming

Tracks stream through an `HTMLAudioElement` (one per playing track, released when it has faded out) routed into the
Web Audio graph with a `MediaElementAudioSourceNode`, so ten tracks never sit decoded in memory:

```
<audio> -> deck gain (crossfades) -> high shelf -> punch peak -> trim -> pause gain -> musicDuck -> musicBus
```

The music therefore still obeys the user's music volume and is still ducked under blasts, warnings and speech.
Crossfades: 1.0 s in, 1.2 s match to match, 1.6 s back to the menu. A track loops if a match outlasts it.

### Endgame escalation (`src/audio/escalation.ts`)

Synchronised to the game clock (`(durationTicks - playTicks) / 60`, read in `handleEvents` every tick; match timing is
not touched). From 30 s remaining an urgency value eases from 0 to 1 (smoothstep, so there is no step at 30 s):

| Remaining | 45 s | 30 s | 15 s | 0 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Playback rate (pitch preserved) | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.045 | 1.090 |
| High shelf at 3.2 kHz | 0 dB | 0 dB | +1.75 dB | +3.5 dB |
| Punch peak at 110 Hz | 0 dB | 0 dB | +1 dB | +2 dB |
| Compensating trim | 0 dB | 0 dB | -0.9 dB | -1.8 dB |

- Tempo uses `preservesPitch = true` (or the `webkit` / `moz` prefixed property). Where pitch cannot be preserved the
  rate is **never** changed; only the brightness/drive lift applies. Rates are written in 0.0025 steps (36 writes
  over the last 30 s), not every frame.
- The escalation starts with the mandatory floor traps: wave 1 goes live at 0:30 (urgency starts rising), wave 2 at
  0:15 (rate about 1.045), and Wahala Crush's last tiles close towards 0:00 (rate 1.09). The trap alarm and the floor
  giving way also duck the music briefly (`buses.duck('warning' | 'blast')`), so each wave lands as a beat.
- The trim is an estimate meant to keep the escalated music at or below its normal level; it has not been checked by
  ear. Effects and warnings are on other buses and untouched; ducks still apply.
- Match end: the tempo eases back to 1.0 (0.06 per second), the tone resets and the track settles to 55% until the
  results screen (`stopMusic`) fades it out.
- Pause (`MatchController.setPaused` -> `audio.setPaused`): a 120 ms fade, then the stream pauses; resume continues
  the same track from the same point. Hiding the tab pauses the match, so the music pauses too.
- Mute: the master fades to 0 as before; the music keeps following the clock, so unmuting lands on the right tempo.
- Rematch: a new track, tempo 1.0, tone reset, un-paused. Quit to title (also from the pause menu): the match track
  fades out and its stream is released.

## Adding the founder's audio later

**Music**: see "Music: the approved playlist" above. Manifest entries (`version: 2`) look like

```json
{ "id": "pixabay_569473", "title": "...", "artist": null, "source": "https://pixabay.com/music/...",
  "licence": "Pixabay Content License", "file": "pixabay_569473.mp3", "approved": true }
```

Ids and file names must match `[A-Za-z0-9_-]+` (`.mp3`, `.ogg` or `.m4a`); entries without a licence are ignored. An
optional `gain` (0..2) trims a track's loudness. The old fixed slots (`menu_loop`, `match_loop`, `match_tension`,
music-file stings) are gone; the short result stingers are the synthesised `win_sting` / `lose_sting` / `draw_sting`
effects.

**Voices** (`src/audio/pidgin.ts`, triggers in `src/audio/pidginReactions.ts`). Put a recording in
`public/audio/voices/recorded/` and list it in `public/audio/voices/recorded.json` with a licence/consent statement
(required; entries without one are ignored). An `id` from `voices.json` gives that catalogue line a voice; a new
`id` with `category` + `text` adds a line. See `public/audio/voices/recorded/README.md` and the line catalogue in
`docs/VOICE_RECORDING_SCRIPT.md`.

Playback rules (unit-tested): per-category cooldowns of 8-30 s; at least 4.5 s between unforced reaction lines; one
reaction voice at a time; shuffle-bag choice with a 40 s recent-line memory; the previous speaker is avoided;
duplicate triggers within 0.6 s collapse to one; ambient conversations wait 2.5 s after any reaction; beds and music
duck while a line is spoken.

**Ambience and effect overrides** (`src/audio/samples.ts`). Any synthesised sound id (including the beds
`crowd_loop`, `buspark_loop`, `crowd_loop_hype`) can be replaced by a licensed file listed in
`public/audio/slots.json`; see `public/audio/README.md` for the slot table.

## Verification

`tests/audio/`: preferences (defaults, persistence, corruption, clamping), playlist (`playlist.test.ts`: no immediate
repeat over hundreds of matches, arena lists, signature priority and fallback, missing files, manifest validation),
escalation curve (`escalation.test.ts`: values at 45/30/15/0 s, smoothness, no rate change without pitch
preservation, compensating trim), streaming player (`music.test.ts`, with fake `<audio>` elements from
`fakeMedia.ts`: probing, skipping missing or broken files, routing into the music duck, `preservesPitch`, tempo steps,
match end, pause/resume, rematch reset, release), engine-level music (`engineMusic.test.ts`: arena pick, clock-driven
tempo also while muted, pause, quit, ducks), voice director (cooldowns, shuffle bag, variety, dedupe, one voice at a
time, ambient scheduling, off switch, recording overrides) against fake servers, reaction mapping, engine
integration, the settings panel, and the rejected-asset guard above. `e2e/audio.spec.ts` checks in a real browser
that no AudioContext exists before the first gesture, that the four sliders persist, that the only audio files ever
requested are approved playlist files listed in the manifest, and that the pause menu pauses the music
(`?debug` exposes `__WAHALA__.music()`). With the real files, Chromium (headless, Playwright) also: fetches and
decodes all nine installed tracks (each longer than 90 s; the #7 slot fails as expected); plays a Danfo Bus Park match
on its starting track `pixabay_504898` with the stream position advancing and `preservesPitch` on; at 0:15 left the
rate is about 1.045 with pitch preserved; pause and resume reach the stream; a Balogun Market match then opens on
`pixabay_573648`; returning to the bus park plays one of its own tracks, never the one just heard, at rate 1.0.
`tools/audio/browserSmoke.mjs` records a bot match through the real engine in headless Chromium.

Not verified: how anything sounds (the container has no speakers; levels, the -14 LUFS trims and the endgame lift
have not been judged by ear) and Safari/iOS playback. Check it by ear: `npm run dev`, play a match, listen to the
last 30 s (the floor traps arrive at 0:30 and 0:15, the music dips under the trap alarm and keeps accelerating),
pause, rematch, switch arenas.
