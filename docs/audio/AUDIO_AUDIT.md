## Headline

**This repository contains no game code and no audio assets.** It holds the design documents and three visual reference files. There is therefore no audio manager, no mixing logic and no playback code to review here. The gameplay build lives in the Architecture session's work, which this session cannot see and was told not to touch. Run `audio-pipeline/audit_repo.sh` against that checkout to complete the inventory; section 4 lists what to look for.

## 3. Audio requirements already written in the design documents

| Source | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Bible section 1 / 11 | Original instrumental loops from Afrobeat, Afrobeats, Highlife and street percussion. Sound events: generator rattle, warning, blast, tyres, horns, Keke, Pidgin shouts, comic elimination sting. |
| Bible section 11 | Natural, varied Nigerian Pidgin crowd reactions, with moments of near-silence so effects read. Hazard warnings stay clear. User volume and mute control. |
| Bible section 3 (PLAYTEST) | Raise crowd/music intensity in the final 30 seconds without drowning danger warnings. |
| Bible section 12 | Music and FX volume in settings. |
| Bible section 17 (legal) | Original or appropriately licensed sound only. Copyright review and original audio licensing are listed as open items. |
| Build prompt item 18 | Original or clearly licensed instrumental rhythm. If assets are unavailable, use WebAudio-generated simple original rhythms, mark them temporary, and never download copyrighted songs. |

Terminology gap: the audio brief for this workstream mentions campaign, bosses, shields, punches and Sudden Death. The Bible describes Wahala Crush (sudden death), Okada protection, push/daze, and no health or boss fights. This workstream delivered cues for both vocabularies; the boss/campaign pack is optional until those features exist.
