Each card has your original recording exactly as received (unedited MP3 bytes) beside the mastered, looped game version. The mastering was measured, not heard: nobody has listened to these masters. Use the A/B button to hear the same moment in both. Audio loads when you press play.

Press a play button to start.

Arena to song map

All seven City Rumble arenas have exactly one district song, so the match for that arena plays it. Two start points (A, B) of the same seamless loop alternate between matches, so a rematch opens somewhere else in the song. Three standard arenas share a district and get a different start point (C) of the same song. Every other arena keeps its Pixabay track. Nothing is shipped: this is what ?audiodraft=match plays.
